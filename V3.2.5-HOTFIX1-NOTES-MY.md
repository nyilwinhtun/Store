# Minimart POS v3.2.5 Hotfix 1

Fixed TypeScript TS7053 in `src/lib/cashierSalesSync.ts` by capturing validated Firestore sale items as `unknown[]` before a callback and validating each item as a record before reading its fields. No `any` cast was introduced; business comparison logic and stored data format were not changed.
